Mailboxes to poll

Receiving reports directly on port 25 needs no credentials and stays the default. Add a mailbox here when that is impossible, or to pick up reports that already collect somewhere. Each domain keeps its own address, so one catch-all mailbox serves every tenant; a single shared address works too, with reports attributed by the domain named inside them. Passwords are encrypted before being stored.
